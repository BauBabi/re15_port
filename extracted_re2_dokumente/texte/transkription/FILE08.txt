SECRETARY'S DIARY B

=== S01 ===
June 8th
As I was straightening up
the chief's room, he burst
through the door with a
furious look on his face.
It's only been 2 months since
I've started working here,
but that's the second time
I've seen him like this.

=== S02 ===
The last time was when I
bumped into that statue, only
this time he looked even more
agitated than ever. I
seriously thought for a
moment that he was going to
hurt me.

=== S03 ===
June 15th
I finally discovered what the
chief has been hiding all
along... If he finds out that
I know, my life will be in
serious danger.
It's getting late already.
I'm just going to have to take
this one day at a time...
